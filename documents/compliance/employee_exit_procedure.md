# Employee Exit Procedure

## Purpose

Defines the steps followed when an employee resigns, from notice
through to final settlement.

## Scope

Applies to all permanent and probationary employees who submit a
resignation notice.

## Process Steps

1. **Resignation notice**: the employee submits their resignation to
   HR, triggering the exit process.
2. **EPF/ETF exit processing**: HR processes the employee's exit from
   the EPF and ETF schemes, following the same statutory framework
   used at enrollment (see EPF/ETF Enrollment & Contribution
   Procedure) — final contributions are calculated and submitted
   before the employee's exit is finalized with the funds.
3. **Final settlement**: before the process is closed, the employee
   receives:
   - **Unused leave payout** — any remaining entitled leave balance,
     settled per the Leave Policy
   - **Final payslip** — reflecting final salary, any outstanding
     overtime, and final EPF/ETF deductions

## Responsibilities

- **Employee**: submits resignation notice
- **HR**: processes EPF/ETF exit, calculates final settlement
- **Finance**: issues the final payslip and leave payout
