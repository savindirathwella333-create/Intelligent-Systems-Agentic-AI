# Payroll Procedure

## Purpose

Defines the monthly payroll cycle, what data feeds into it, and who
gives final approval before disbursement.

## Scope

Applies to all permanent and probationary employees.

## Payroll Cycle

Payroll runs on a **monthly** cycle.

## Payroll Inputs

Each month's payroll is calculated using attendance and overtime data
already recorded through the Attendance Policy and Overtime Policy —
clocked hours from the biometric scanner, plus any approved overtime
at the 1.5x rate. EPF and ETF contributions are calculated per the
EPF/ETF Enrollment & Contribution Procedure and deducted accordingly.

## Approval

Before payroll is disbursed, **Finance** gives final approval,
reviewing the calculated payroll against attendance, overtime, and
statutory deduction records before payment is released.

## Responsibilities

- **HR**: compiles attendance, overtime, and EPF/ETF data into the
  payroll calculation
- **Finance**: reviews and gives final approval before disbursement
