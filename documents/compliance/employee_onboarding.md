# Employee Onboarding

## Purpose

Defines what happens in a new hire's first week — documentation,
access setup, and induction — so onboarding is consistent and nothing
gets missed.

## Scope

Applies to all new permanent and probationary employees.

## Documentation

In the first week, HR collects and processes:

- **NIC** — for identification and EPF registration (see EPF/ETF
  Enrollment & Contribution Procedure)
- **Bank details** — for payroll setup
- **EPF/ETF forms** — completed as part of the existing enrollment
  procedure, within the same first-week window

## Access Setup

Physical access is arranged before or on the employee's start date —
keys and/or an ID card, so the new hire can access the workplace from
day one without depending on someone letting them in.

## Induction

Induction is informal rather than a scheduled orientation program —
the new hire is introduced to their team and shown around by their
supervisor, with role-specific guidance given as needed rather than a
fixed session.

## Responsibilities

- **HR**: collects documentation, processes EPF/ETF enrollment
- **[Role to be named]**: arranges physical access (keys/ID card)
  ahead of the start date
- **Supervisor**: handles informal team introduction and induction
