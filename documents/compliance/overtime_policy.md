# Overtime Policy

## Purpose

Defines how overtime is approved, calculated, and paid.

## Scope

Applies to all permanent and probationary employees eligible for
overtime.

## Approval

Overtime is logged and approved after it occurs, rather than requiring
pre-approval before the work happens. Once logged, it requires
sign-off from both the employee's supervisor and HR before it's
included in payroll.

## Overtime Rate

Overtime is paid at a fixed internal rate of **1.5x standard hourly
pay**, applied to all approved overtime hours regardless of when they
occur.

## Responsibilities

- **Employee**: logs overtime hours worked
- **Supervisor**: reviews and approves logged overtime
- **HR**: co-signs approval, ensures the correct rate is applied in
  payroll
