# Attendance Policy

## Purpose

Defines standard working hours, how attendance is recorded, and what
counts as late arrival.

## Scope

Applies to all permanent and probationary employees.

## Working Hours

Standard working hours apply across the business, rather than varying
by role — a single, consistent schedule for all employees.

## Recording Attendance

Attendance is recorded using a **biometric scanner** at the workplace
entrance. Each employee clocks in and out individually, giving an
accurate, tamper-resistant record of actual arrival and departure
times.

## Late Arrival

An employee is considered late as soon as the biometric scan is
logged after the standard start time — there is no grace period built
into this policy.

## Responsibilities

- **Employee**: clocks in/out via the biometric scanner at the start
  and end of each working day
- **HR**: monitors attendance records, follows up on patterns of
  lateness
