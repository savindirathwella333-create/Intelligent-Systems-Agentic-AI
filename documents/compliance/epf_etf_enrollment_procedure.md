# EPF/ETF Enrollment & Contribution Procedure

## Purpose
This procedure describes the internal steps HR follows to enroll a new
employee in the Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) and Employees' Trust
Fund (ETF) schemes, and how monthly contributions are processed.

## Scope
Applies to all permanent and probationary employees, as required under
Sri Lankan labour law.

## Enrollment Steps
1. **On joining**: HR collects the employee's NIC number and completes
   the EPF member registration form within the first week of employment.
2. **Fund registration**: the completed form and employee details are
   submitted to the Employees' Provident Fund Department to obtain a
   member number.
3. **Payroll setup**: the employee's EPF/ETF details are entered into
   the payroll system before the first salary cycle, so deductions
   begin correctly from month one.

## Monthly Contribution Process
- Contribution rates follow the schedule set out in the current EPF
  Act and ETF Act — HR verifies the applicable rate each year rather
  than relying on a fixed internal figure, since statutory rates can
  change.
- Contributions are calculated on total monthly earnings as defined by
  the Act (basic salary plus specified allowances).
- Deductions are remitted to the respective funds by the statutory
  deadline each month, alongside the employer's own contribution.

## Recordkeeping
- Each employee's contribution history is retained in their personnel
  file for the duration of employment and per the statutory retention
  period after exit.
- Discrepancies between payroll deductions and fund receipts are
  reconciled by HR within the same month they're identified.

## Responsibilities
- **HR**: registration, payroll setup, annual rate verification
- **Finance**: monthly remittance to EPF/ETF, reconciliation
- **Employee**: providing accurate NIC and personal details at onboarding
