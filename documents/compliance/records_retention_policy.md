# Records Retention Policy

## Purpose

Defines how long different categories of employee records are kept,
to meet statutory requirements while avoiding indefinite retention of
personal data.

## Scope

Applies to payroll, EPF/ETF, attendance, and grievance records for all
current and former employees.

## Retention by Category

- **Payroll records** — retained for the period required under
  applicable statutory record-keeping requirements, verified against
  current labour law rather than a fixed internal figure, since
  requirements can change.
- **EPF/ETF records** — retained per the statutory retention period
  tied to the EPF and ETF Acts (see EPF/ETF Enrollment & Contribution
  Procedure), verified rather than hardcoded for the same reason.
- **Attendance records** — retained for a period sufficient to support
  payroll and overtime verification, aligned with the payroll record
  retention period above.
- **Grievance records** — retained for a period sufficient to cover
  any potential follow-up or dispute, reviewed against statutory
  requirements where applicable.

## Responsibility for Verification

Because statutory retention periods can change, HR verifies the
current applicable period for each category periodically, rather than
relying on a fixed number written into this policy — the same approach
used for the EPF contribution rate elsewhere in these documents.

## Responsibilities

- **HR**: maintains records for each category per the verified
  retention period, securely disposes of records once the period
  lapses
