# Internal Leave Policy

## Purpose

Describes annual, casual, and medical leave entitlements for
employees, and the process for requesting and approving leave.

## Scope

Applies to all permanent and probationary employees.

## Leave Entitlements

- **Annual leave**: accrued monthly, available for planned time off.
- **Casual leave**: for short-notice personal matters, subject to a
  yearly cap.
- **Medical leave**: requires supporting documentation for absences
  beyond 2 consecutive days.

## Request & Approval Process

1. Employee submits a leave request to their supervisor with at least
   3 working days' notice for planned leave (not required for medical
   leave).
2. Supervisor approves or declines based on team coverage.
3. HR updates the employee's leave balance once approved.

## Unused Leave

- Unused annual leave may be carried forward up to a limit set by
  company policy, reviewed annually.
- On resignation, any unused entitled leave is settled in the final
  payslip per statutory requirements.

## Responsibilities

- **Employee**: submits requests with adequate notice
- **Supervisor**: approves/declines based on coverage
- **HR**: maintains leave balance records
