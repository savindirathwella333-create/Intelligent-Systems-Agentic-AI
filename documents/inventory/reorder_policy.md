# Raw Material Reorder Policy

## Purpose
This policy defines when and how raw materials (oils, lye, fragrance
compounds, packaging materials) are reordered to prevent production
stoppages while avoiding excess inventory holding costs.

## Scope
Applies to all raw materials used in soap and cosmetic product batches
across the production facility.

## Reorder Thresholds
- Reorder is triggered when stock falls below **14 days** of average
  consumption, based on the trailing 30-day usage average.
- Fast-moving materials (base oils, packaging) use a 14-day threshold.
- Slow-moving materials (specialty fragrances, niche additives) use a
  30-day threshold, since supplier lead times are longer.

## Responsibilities
- **Inventory Officer**: monitors stock levels weekly, flags items
  crossing the reorder threshold.
- **Procurement**: places purchase orders within 2 working days of a
  flagged item.
- **Production Lead**: notified if any material drops below 5 days of
  stock, to plan around potential delays.

## Related Records
Reorder decisions are logged in the weekly stock report, including
item, quantity ordered, supplier, and expected delivery date.
