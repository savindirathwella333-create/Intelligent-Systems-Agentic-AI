# Warehouse Safety

## Purpose

Defines basic safety practices for storing raw materials, packaging,
and finished goods, covering physical, chemical, and fire safety
risks relevant to a soap and cosmetics manufacturing warehouse.

## Scope

Applies to all storage areas within the warehouse.

## Storage Safety Rules

- **Stacking limits** — palletized goods are stacked within safe
  height limits to prevent collapse; heavier items are stored on
  lower shelves.
- **Chemical separation** — lye, fragrance compounds, and other
  reactive materials are stored separately from each other and from
  finished goods, following the relevant material safety guidance for
  each substance.
- **Fire safety** — flammable materials are stored away from heat
  sources and electrical equipment; fire extinguishers and clear
  emergency exits are maintained and not obstructed by stored goods.

## Reporting Issues

Safety is handled on a report-as-found basis rather than a fixed
routine inspection schedule — any staff member who notices a hazard
(unstable stacking, a chemical storage issue, a blocked exit, etc.)
reports it immediately rather than waiting for a scheduled check.

## Responsibilities

- **Inventory Officer**: primary point of contact for reporting
  warehouse safety issues, given regular presence in storage areas
- **Manager**: ensures reported issues are actioned and resolved
