# Supplier Management Policy

## Purpose

Defines how suppliers are approved, reviewed, and removed, to ensure
the business sources raw materials and packaging from consistently
reliable partners.

## Scope

Applies to all suppliers of raw materials, packaging, and related
production inputs.

## Approving a New Supplier

A new supplier is approved based on a combination of factors, not any
single one alone:

- **Price competitiveness** relative to existing suppliers for similar
  materials
- **Delivery reliability** — ability to meet agreed lead times
- **Quality consistency** — samples or trial orders meet the
  business's quality requirements before full approval

A supplier is only added to the approved supplier list once all three
factors have been reviewed and found acceptable.

## Ongoing Review

Existing suppliers are reviewed based on order volume rather than a
fixed calendar schedule — high-volume suppliers are reviewed more
frequently than low-volume, occasional ones, since more transactions
give a clearer picture of actual performance. Each review considers:

- On-time delivery rate over the review period
- Consistency of quality across deliveries
- Responsiveness to issues raised (e.g. damaged stock, discrepancies)

## Removing a Supplier

A supplier is removed from the approved list when they have a pattern
of bad deliveries — repeated late, incomplete, or quality-failing
deliveries, rather than a single isolated incident. Each bad delivery
is logged against the supplier's record (see Damaged Stock Policy for
how individual incidents are recorded), and a pattern of these across
the review period is what triggers removal, not a one-off failure.

## Responsibilities

- **Procurement**: evaluates new suppliers, conducts ongoing reviews,
  makes removal decisions
- **Inventory Officer**: logs delivery issues that feed into supplier
  reviews
