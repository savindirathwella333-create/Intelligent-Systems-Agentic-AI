# Quality Control Procedure

## Purpose

Defines the quality checks applied before, during, and after
production, so every batch meets consistent standards before it
reaches packaging and dispatch.

## Scope

Applies to all production batches across product lines.

## Pre-Production Checks

Before a batch begins, checks go beyond just inspecting the raw
materials themselves:

- **Raw material inspection** — condition, correct batch/expiry
  matching what was verified at receiving
- **Equipment check** — production equipment is clean and functioning
  correctly before use
- **Recipe/formulation confirmation** — the correct formulation and
  quantities are confirmed against the production plan before mixing
  begins

## In-Process Checks

During production, batches are checked for **pH**, taken at a defined
point in the mixing process to confirm the formulation is within the
expected range before the batch continues. An out-of-range reading
halts the batch for review before it proceeds further.

## Post-Production Checks

Before a completed batch is approved for packaging and dispatch, it's
checked for:

- **Weight** — individual units checked against the expected weight
  range
- **Appearance** — visual check for consistency (color, texture,
  surface defects)

A batch only moves to Finished Goods Dispatch once it passes both
checks.

## Responsibilities

- **Production Lead**: pre-production and in-process checks
- **Quality Control staff**: post-production weight and appearance
  checks, final batch approval
