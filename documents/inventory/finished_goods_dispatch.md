# Finished Goods Dispatch

## Purpose

Defines the steps a completed batch goes through after production —
release, packaging, and final checks — before it leaves for delivery
or sale.

## Scope

Applies to all finished goods that have passed post-production quality
checks (see Quality Control Procedure).

## Release Approval

A batch that has passed its post-production weight and appearance
checks does not move straight to packaging — it first requires a
separate release/approval step, confirming the batch is formally
cleared before it's handed off for packaging.

## Packaging

Once released, batches are handed to a separate packaging team
(distinct from Production), who package the goods according to the
product's packaging and labeling requirements.

## Final Check Before Dispatch

Before packaged goods actually leave the facility, a final check is
carried out — confirming the packaged goods match the original order
(item, quantity) and that labeling is correct — before dispatch is
approved.

## Responsibilities

- **Production Lead**: confirms batch release approval after quality
  checks pass
- **Packaging team**: packages released batches per product
  requirements
- **[Role to be named]**: performs the final pre-dispatch check
