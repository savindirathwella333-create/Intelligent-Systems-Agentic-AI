# Stock Receiving Procedure

## Purpose

Defines how incoming raw materials and packaging are inspected and
recorded when they arrive at the facility, so stock records stay
accurate and damaged or incorrect deliveries are caught early.

## Scope

Applies to all incoming raw material and packaging deliveries from
approved suppliers.

## Receiving Steps

1. **Delivery check-in**: the Inventory Officer confirms the delivery
   matches the purchase order (item, quantity, supplier) before
   unloading.
2. **Quality inspection**: items are visually inspected for damage,
   correct labeling, and expiry/batch dates where applicable.
3. **Quantity verification**: received quantity is counted and
   compared against the delivery note and purchase order.
4. **System update**: verified quantities are entered into the stock
   system on the same day as receipt.

## Discrepancy Handling

- Under-delivery or damaged items are logged and reported to
  Procurement within 1 working day.
- Rejected items are held separately and not entered into usable
  stock until the supplier issue is resolved.

## Responsibilities

- **Inventory Officer**: check-in, inspection, system update
- **Procurement**: resolves supplier discrepancies
