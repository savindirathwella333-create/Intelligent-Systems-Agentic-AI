# Purchase Order Procedure

## Purpose

Defines who can raise a purchase order (PO), what approval it needs
before going to a supplier, and how incoming deliveries are matched
back to it.

## Scope

Applies to all purchase orders for raw materials, packaging, and
related production inputs.

## Raising a PO

A PO can be raised by the Inventory Officer or other authorized staff
who identify a need — this isn't limited to Procurement alone, since
the Inventory Officer is often the first to notice a reorder threshold
has been crossed (see Raw Material Reorder Policy).

## Approval

Any PO above a set value threshold requires manager sign-off before it
can be sent to a supplier. POs below that threshold can be issued
directly by the person who raised them, to avoid slowing down routine,
low-value reorders.

## Matching Deliveries to POs

When a delivery arrives, it's matched back to its original PO by PO
number, referenced against the delivery note. This check happens as
part of the existing Stock Receiving Procedure — the Inventory Officer
confirms the delivery matches the PO (item, quantity, supplier) during
check-in, before the quality inspection and quantity verification
steps in that procedure.

## Responsibilities

- **Inventory Officer / authorized staff**: raise POs when a need is
  identified
- **Manager**: approves POs above the value threshold
- **Inventory Officer**: matches deliveries to POs during receiving
