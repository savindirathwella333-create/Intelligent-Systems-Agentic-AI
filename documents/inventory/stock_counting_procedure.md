# Stock Counting Procedure

## Purpose

Defines how physical stock counts are carried out and verified against
system records, so inventory data stays accurate and discrepancies are
caught and resolved quickly.

## Scope

Applies to all raw materials, packaging, and finished goods held in
the warehouse.

## Counting Schedule

- **Weekly spot-checks**: fast-moving items (base oils, packaging) are
  spot-checked weekly, covering a rotating sample of stock rather than
  the full inventory each time.
- **Monthly full count**: a complete physical count of all inventory
  is carried out once a month, covering every item regardless of
  movement speed.

## Who Performs the Count

Counts are carried out by a **Stock Verifier**, a role kept separate
from the Inventory Officer who maintains the day-to-day system
records. This separation ensures the person checking stock isn't the
same person who logged it, so errors or discrepancies are more likely
to be caught.

## Handling Variances

1. Any variance between the physical count and the system record is
   logged, however small.
2. A re-count is performed on the affected item before anything else
   happens, to rule out a simple counting error.
3. If the variance persists after the re-count, it's escalated:
   - Variances **under 2%** of the item's counted value are logged and
     reviewed at the next weekly stock meeting.
   - Variances **2% or greater** trigger an immediate investigation by
     the Inventory Officer, checking recent transactions (receiving,
     dispatch, production usage) for the item.
4. Confirmed discrepancies are corrected in the system only after the
   investigation concludes, with the reason recorded.

## Responsibilities

- **Stock Verifier**: performs weekly spot-checks and the monthly full
  count, independent of daily record-keeping
- **Inventory Officer**: investigates escalated variances, corrects
  records once resolved
